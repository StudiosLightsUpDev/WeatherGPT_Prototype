WeatherGPT Prototype 🌤️
WeatherGPT is an intelligent, conversational layer built on top of raw weather data. It is designed to make weather forecasting intuitively understandable and instantly actionable for local communities. Rather than simply displaying temperatures and precipitation percentages, WeatherGPT acts as a proactive assistant and emergency guide.

The Problem
Traditional weather applications provide raw data that users must interpret themselves. During critical weather conditions or natural emergencies, standard alerts often cause panic without offering a solution. WeatherGPT bridges this gap by transforming data into clear, conversational advice and actionable safety protocols.

Core Features
1. Conversational & Actionable Insights
WeatherGPT doesn't just tell you the forecast; it tells you how to prepare.

Rainy Conditions: Automatically suggests packing an umbrella, wearing a raincoat, or preparing specific gear before you step out.

Extreme Heat: Prompts users to stay hydrated, suggests purchasing glucose, and advises on sun protection.

2. Intelligent Crowd Management (Core USP) 🚨
In severe or critical weather conditions, generic alerts can lead to chaos. WeatherGPT's core innovation is its crowd management routing.

Dynamic Safe-Routing: Instead of just warning users about a severe storm, the system actively guides individuals to the nearest designated safe zones.

Havoc Reduction: By managing this at a bulk level, WeatherGPT prevents bottlenecks, reduces panic, and significantly mitigates the chances of havoc during city-wide emergencies.

3. Route-Based Trip Planning 🗺️
Planning a road trip? Tell WeatherGPT your destination. If the location is safe to visit, the system will map out the weather conditions for every point along your route. This ensures you are never caught off guard by sudden weather shifts during your journey.

Interactive Demonstration: The Canva AI Simulation 🎮
To visually demonstrate the real-world impact of our crowd management and weather response system, we have built a simple interactive game using Canva AI.

How it works:

Sunny Mode: Trigger this, and the characters in the simulation quickly move outside to enjoy the open area.

Rain/Critical Mode: Trigger this, and the system instantly routes all characters back to the safety of their homes etc.

This gamified simulation acts as a micro-level representation of WeatherGPT's macro-level goal: seamlessly shifting populations to safety based on real-time environmental data.
